About PEVS

A physics-governed virtual laboratory for validating propulsion concepts under real conservation laws.

What is PEVS?

P.E.V.S (Physics Engine Validation Simulator) is a virtual propulsion laboratory designed to simulate spacecraft motion while strictly enforcing fundamental physical laws.

Unlike traditional simulations that focus only on kinematics or visual motion, PEVS treats physics as a first-class constraint. Every engine, environment, and interaction must explicitly account for energy, momentum, and mass exchange.

Why This Project Exists

The internet is filled with propulsion concepts that claim thrust without reaction mass, motion without force exchange, or energy extraction from nowhere. Many of these ideas are presented with convincing animations but lack physical accountability.

PEVS exists to answer a single, non-negotiable question:

“If the system moved, what did it push against?”

Core Design Principles

Conservation First

Energy, momentum, and mass are tracked continuously. Any unexplained drift is flagged automatically.

Closed-System Discipline

Engines cannot secretly interact with the environment. All external fields must be explicitly defined.

Engine–Environment Separation

Engines generate interactions. Environments respond. The two are modeled independently to prevent hidden coupling.

No Assumptions of Impossibility

PEVS does not declare an engine impossible by design. It only evaluates whether the math remains consistent.

How the Simulation Works (High Level)

  1. User defines an engine model and environment
  2. The system evolves step-by-step using numerical integration
  3. All forces, fields, and reactions are logged
  4. Conservation laws are audited continuously
  5. A verdict is generated based on physical consistency

What PEVS Does Not Claim

  • It does not prove real-world feasibility
  • It does not replace experimental validation
  • It does not assume speculative physics is correct
  • It does not allow violations of conservation laws

Project Scope

This project was developed as a final project for CS50X: Introduction to Computer Science, and is designed to be extended beyond the course.

The long-term vision is to evolve PEVS into a modular, extensible physics validation platform for education, research, and speculative-engine analysis.
