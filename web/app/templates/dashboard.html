What is PEVS?

PEVS is a physics-driven virtual propulsion laboratory designed to evaluate propulsion concepts under strict conservation laws.

Every simulation is numerically integrated, audited for energy and momentum conservation, and automatically classified as physically valid or invalid.

Reactionless propulsion claims are treated as test subjects, not assumptions.
