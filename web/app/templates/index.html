What is PEVS?

PEVS is a physics-driven simulation platform designed to test propulsion concepts inside a closed, controlled virtual environment that strictly obeys fundamental conservation laws.

Every simulation is evaluated against fundamental conservation laws to distinguish physically valid engines from impossible or fraudulent claims.

Every simulation is executed step-by-step, with full accounting of energy, momentum, mass, and field interactions. If a concept violates physics, the system does not ignore it, it flags it.

Conservation-Law Enforcement

Energy, momentum, and mass are tracked at every simulation step. Violations are detected automatically with full numerical audits, quantified, and reported.

Audit & Fraud Detection

Closed-system violations, hidden energy sources, and undeclared momentum exchanges are automatically flagged.

Environment vs Engine Separation

Propulsion systems and environments are strictly separated. Engines cannot secretly push against “nothing”.

External fields, radiation, and gravity are modeled independently from engines, preventing hidden momentum or energy injection.

Reactionless Claim Detection

Engines that attempt motion without declared exchange are flagged and analyzed step-by-step.

Reactionless and field-based engines are allowed, but only if their momentum and energy exchange is physically valid.

Field Interaction Support

Legitimate field-based interactions are allowed, provided their energy and momentum exchanges are explicitly conserved.

How It Works

  1. Define the environment (vacuum, gravity field, external sources)
  2. Configure the propulsion system and initial state
  3. Run a time-resolved physics simulation
  4. Audit conservation laws and field exchanges
  5. Review results, drift analysis, and verdict

Who is this for?

P.E.V.S is built for engineers, physics enthusiasts, researchers, and curious minds who want to test ideas, not just imagine them.

  • Physics and aerospace students
  • Researchers evaluating speculative propulsion concepts
  • Engineers testing conservation-compliant motion models
  • Educators demonstrating real-world physics constraints

Philosophy

“If a propulsion concept works, conservation laws will show it. If it doesn’t, the simulator will.”

PEVS does not assume an engine is impossible. It simply asks one question:

“Where did the momentum and energy come from?”
